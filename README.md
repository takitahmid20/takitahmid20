## 06 / Research

### BanglaRAG

**Enhancing Bangla Language Models through Retrieval-Augmented Generation**

My research focuses on building a **Bangla-first RAG pipeline** rather than depending entirely on translation-based approaches.

```text
Data Sources
      ↓
Crawling
      ↓
Cleaning & Deduplication
      ↓
Chunking
      ↓
Embeddings
      ↓
Vector Database
      ↓
Retrieval
      ↓
Bangla Language Model
```

The broader goal is to explore how retrieval can improve the **grounding, relevance and usefulness of AI systems for Bangla content**.
