### `06` — Research

#### BanglaRAG

**Enhancing Bangla Language Models through Retrieval-Augmented Generation**

My research explores a **Bangla-first RAG approach** rather than relying entirely on translation-based pipelines.

Current exploration includes:

```text
Data Sources
     ↓
Crawling
     ↓
Cleaning & Deduplication
     ↓
Chunking
     ↓
Embedding
     ↓
Vector Database
     ↓
Retrieval
     ↓
Bangla LLM
```

The goal is to investigate how retrieval can improve the usefulness and factual grounding of language models for Bangla content.

### `07` — How I think about building

I care less about collecting technologies and more about understanding **why something should exist**.

My usual process looks something like:

```text
Problem
  ↓
Understand
  ↓
Design
  ↓
Prototype
  ↓
Engineer
  ↓
Test
  ↓
Ship
  ↓
Improve
```

A beautiful interface without a solid system is incomplete.

A technically impressive system with a terrible experience is also incomplete.

**Good products need both.**
